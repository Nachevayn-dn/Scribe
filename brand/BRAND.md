# MedicDesk.ai — Brand Foundations

Companion document to the visual brand canvas (logo, color/type system, social
mockups, competitive landscape, pricing framework): see the artifact linked
from the PR/session, or regenerate it from `brand/` design source if the team
adopts the `design` skill workflow. This file is the text-only reference —
mission, vision, positioning, voice, and the pricing matrix — for anyone who
needs it without opening a canvas.

Tone brief this brand was built against: **clinical trust and premium
clinical-grade — solution-oriented, trusted partner.** Not a hype-forward AI
vendor; not a cold enterprise incumbent either.

## Mission

> We build the AI front-and-back office for healthcare — starting with
> ambient documentation and extending to every inbound and outbound call —
> so clinical time stays clinical.

## Vision

> A future where no clinician's day is shaped by a keyboard or a hold queue —
> where a trusted, intelligent AI desk runs quietly behind every visit, every
> call, and every chain of care, and the administrative half of healthcare
> simply disappears into software.

## Positioning statement

**"The desk behind every visit."**

MedicDesk.ai is the single vendor for the AI work a practice's front desk and
back office does all day: listening to the visit and writing the note
(Ambient Scribe), answering every call in (Inbound Agent), and making every
call out (Outbound Agent) — sold as one connected system with transparent
pricing for a single clinic and negotiated pricing that scales to a chain.

Competitors either sell enterprise-only documentation (Dragon Copilot,
Abridge, Ambience) with no self-serve path, or cheap self-serve scribing
(Freed, Heidi) with no voice-agent story, or voice agents alone (Hippocratic,
Assort, Infinitus) with no note. MedicDesk.ai is the only one selling both
sides of the desk under one roof. Full competitive detail lives in
`Competitive overview/README.md`.

## Brand pillars (voice)

1. **Clinical-grade, always** — earn trust with specifics (accuracy rates,
   audit trails, EHR fit), never adjectives. If we can't cite it, we don't
   claim it.
2. **Quietly capable** — the best praise is "I forgot it was even there."
   No hype, no exclamation points, no growth-hacker slang.
3. **Partner, not vendor** — "we" language, long horizon; talk about the
   practice's outcomes before our own roadmap.
4. **Plain-spoken** — a front-desk lead and a CMIO should both understand
   every sentence on first read. No stacked jargon.

**We say:** "Signed notes in your EHR, same day." · "98.6% clinical-entity
accuracy, audited quarterly." · "One desk. Every visit, every call." · "You
keep the last word — every note is yours to edit."

**We don't say:** "Revolutionary AI that changes everything!" · "Replace
your staff with AI." · unqualified "99.9% accurate" · emoji or
exclamation-heavy copy.

Per-product lines: **Scribe** — "Every word charted, nothing dictated."
**Inbound Agent** — "The line always picks up." **Outbound Agent** —
"No-shows, called and closed."

## Visual identity summary

- **Mark**: a rounded-square tile with three rising bars (a waveform — the
  spoken visit, the spoken call) and a circular checkmark badge (the moment
  it becomes a signed note or a resolved call). No stethoscopes, crosses, or
  clip-art medicine.
- **Wordmark**: `medicdesk.ai`, lowercase, set in Source Serif 4 Semibold.
- **Color**: deep ink navy (primary), a warm brass accent (premium/CTA,
  the Scribe line), a muted teal accent (trust, the voice-agent line —
  never mixed with brass on one element), warm off-white neutrals
  throughout (no sterile clinical white/grey).
- **Type**: Source Serif 4 for display/headlines, IBM Plex Sans for body and
  UI, IBM Plex Mono for numerals, labels, and pricing figures.

## Pricing framework

Recommended launch pricing — transparent and self-serve at the single-clinic
tier, negotiated at the chain tier. Usage billed monthly in arrears;
platform/seat fees billed annually (add ~18% for monthly billing). This is
MedicDesk.ai's own go-to-market recommendation, not a published competitor
rate.

| Product | Single Clinic <br>(1–5 providers, self-serve) | Growth / Group <br>(6–25 providers, up to 3 sites) | Chain / Health System <br>(25+ providers, multi-site) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ambient Scribe** <br>*per provider / month* | **$219** — unlimited encounters, 1 EHR export format, email support | **$179** — bi-directional EHR sync, custom note templates, priority support | **from $139**, volume-tiered — site-level rollout, dedicated CSM, SLA |
| **Inbound Agent** <br>scheduling, intake, triage, reception | **$249/mo** + $0.95 per resolved call after 300/mo included | **$549/mo** + $0.75 per resolved call after 1,200/mo included | **Custom** platform fee + $0.45–$0.65 per resolved call at volume, per-site numbers |
| **Outbound Agent** <br>reminders, recall, follow-up, prior-auth, balance | **$149/mo** + $0.65 per completed contact after 400/mo included | **$349/mo** + $0.50 per completed contact after 1,500/mo included | **Custom** platform fee + $0.30–$0.40 per completed contact at volume |
| **MedicDesk Complete** <br>all three, bundled | **–15%** off combined list, one invoice | **–20%** off combined list, one invoice, one CSM | **Negotiated** — single MSA across all sites and products |

**Onboarding**: Single Clinic — self-serve, live same week. Growth — guided
setup, included. Chain — dedicated implementation team, phased multi-site
rollout.

**Contract terms**: Single Clinic — month-to-month, cancel anytime. Growth —
annual, with a 30-day pilot. Chain — multi-year MSA with volume step-downs.

**Trust & compliance**: HIPAA BAA on every tier. SOC 2 Type II, audit-logged
calls and encounters, EHR-native identity at Growth and above.

### Where these numbers sit against the market

- Ambient Scribe undercuts the enterprise incumbents (DAX/Dragon Copilot
  ~$369–$830, Suki ~$299–$399, DeepScribe ~$400–$750) while pricing above
  the bare self-serve tier (Freed $39–$119, Heidi ~$150) — the gap MedicDesk
  fills is "enterprise-grade trust without enterprise-grade friction."
- Inbound/Outbound Agent pricing is usage-based like the market's voice-agent
  players (Hippocratic ~$9/agent-hour, Infinitus platform+usage) but
  published and self-serve at the Single Clinic and Growth tiers, where
  Assort, Hippocratic, and Infinitus all gate behind a demo request.
- The bundle discount is the wedge no competitor can match today: nobody
  else sells scribe + inbound + outbound as one product line.
